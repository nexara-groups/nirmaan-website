Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data, and your rights under Indian law.

Last updated: 7 July 2026

Sai Nirmaan Architects (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through our website sainirmaanarchitects.com, how we use and protect it, and the rights available to you under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other applicable Indian law. In this policy, we act as the Data Fiduciary that determines how and why your personal data is processed.

1. Information we collect

We only collect data you provide or that is necessary to operate the site:

  • Enquiry details: when you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and the content of your message.
  • Usage data: if you consent to analytics cookies, we collect anonymised information about how you use the site (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type) via Google Analytics.
  • Consent record: your cookie choices are stored locally in your browser so we can honour them on future visits.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, and we do not require you to create an account.

2. How and why we use it

We process your personal data for the following purposes, on the lawful basis of your consent (which you give by submitting the enquiry form or accepting cookies) and our legitimate interest in operating the practice:

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the consultation or services you request.
  • To communicate with you about your project.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it (only with analytics consent).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, or advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Analytics cookies load only after you consent via our cookie banner; until then, Google Analytics runs in a consent-denied state and does not store identifiers. You can change your choice at any time using the link in the footer. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with trusted service providers (Data Processors) who help us operate the site, under appropriate safeguards:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): website usage analytics, only with your consent.
  • Web3Forms: processes and delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting and delivery.

We may also disclose data where required by law or a lawful government request. We never sell your data to third parties.

5. Data retention

We keep enquiry data only for as long as necessary to respond to and manage your enquiry and any resulting engagement, and thereafter as required for our legitimate business or legal records. Analytics data is retained by Google in line with our Google Analytics configuration (typically up to 14 months). When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Correction and updating: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your personal data where it is no longer required.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (for cookies, use the Cookie Preferences link; for enquiries, contact us).
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint about how we handle your data.
  • Nominate: nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10. We will respond within a reasonable period in accordance with the law. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate your complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authority of any significant personal-data breach as required by law.

8. Children’s data

Our website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Some of our processors (such as Google) may process data on servers located outside India. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer and on transfers permitted under the DPDP Act.
