Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data, and your rights under Indian law.

Last updated: 7 July 2026

Sai Nirmaan Architects (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect through our website sainirmaanarchitects.com, how we use and protect it, and the rights and responsibilities that apply to you under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), read with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and other applicable Indian law. In this policy, we act as the Data Fiduciary that determines how and why your personal data is processed, and we have designated a Grievance Officer (see section 11) to address your requests and complaints.

1. Information we collect

We only collect data you provide or that is necessary to operate the site:

  • Enquiry details: when you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (optional), and the content of your message.
  • Usage data: if you consent to analytics cookies, we collect anonymised information about how you use the site (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type) via Google Analytics.
  • Consent record: your cookie choices are stored locally in your browser so we can honour them on future visits.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, and we do not require you to create an account.

2. How and why we use it

We process your personal data for the following purposes, on the lawful basis of your consent (which you give by submitting the enquiry form or accepting cookies) and our legitimate interest in operating the practice:

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the consultation or services you request.
  • To communicate with you about your project.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it (only with analytics consent).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Your consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous, given through a clear affirmative action and limited to the personal data needed for these purposes. You may withdraw it at any time (see section 6); withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out before the withdrawal. We do not use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, or advertising, and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Analytics cookies load only after you consent via our cookie banner; until then, Google Analytics runs in a consent-denied state and does not store identifiers. You can change your choice at any time using the link in the footer. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with trusted service providers (Data Processors) who help us operate the site, under appropriate safeguards:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): website usage analytics, only with your consent.
  • Web3Forms: processes and delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting and delivery.

We may also disclose data where required by law or a lawful government request. We never sell your data to third parties.

5. Data retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected. In practice:

  • Enquiry data is retained for as long as necessary to respond to and manage your enquiry and any resulting engagement, and in any case no longer than three years from our last interaction with you — unless a longer period is required by law (for example, tax, accounting, or contractual records).
  • Analytics data is retained by Google in line with our Google Analytics configuration (currently up to 14 months).
  • Your consent record stays in your browser until you clear it or change your choice.

When personal data is no longer required, or when you withdraw consent and no other lawful basis applies, we securely delete or anonymise it.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

  • Access: obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we process it, and the parties with whom we have shared it.
  • Correction, completion and updating: ask us to correct inaccurate or misleading data and complete or update incomplete data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your personal data where it is no longer required for the purpose it was collected.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (for cookies, use the link; for enquiries, contact our Grievance Officer).
  • Grievance redressal: have your complaint about our handling of your data addressed by our Grievance Officer.
  • Nominate: nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (section 11). We will acknowledge your request promptly and respond within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act. There is no charge for a reasonable request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may make a complaint to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Your responsibilities as a Data Principal

The DPDP Act also places duties on you. When you interact with us, you agree to:

  • Provide information that is accurate and complete, and not impersonate another person when sharing your details.
  • Not suppress any material information where it is legally required to be disclosed.
  • Not raise a false or frivolous grievance or complaint.
  • Provide only verifiably authentic information when exercising your right to correction or erasure.

Filing a false or frivolous complaint may attract a penalty under the Act.

8. Data security and breach notification

We apply reasonable security safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse — including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, access controls, and limiting who within our practice can access enquiry data. While no method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure, in the event of a personal-data breach that is likely to affect you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India in the manner and within the timelines required under the DPDP Act.

9. Children’s data

Our website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, and we do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Some of our processors (such as Google) may process data on servers located outside India. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer and on transfers permitted under the DPDP Act. We will not transfer your data to any country or territory that the Central Government restricts under the Act.
