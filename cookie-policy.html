Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, and how to control them.

Last updated: 7 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Sai Nirmaan Architects uses cookies and similar technologies on sainirmaanarchitects.com. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites work, remember your preferences, and understand how they are used. Similar technologies (such as your browser’s local storage) work in comparable ways.

Your consent

When you first visit our site, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Non-essential cookies load only after you consent. We use Google Consent Mode, which keeps analytics in a consent-denied state until you agree, so no analytics identifiers are stored beforehand. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the link in the footer.

Managing cookies

  • Use the link in the footer to accept or reject analytics at any time.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the site works.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics across all sites using Google’s browser opt-out add-on.
