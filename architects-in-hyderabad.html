Architects in Hyderabad

Our Telangana studio is at Tellapur, near the Alien Space Station.

Our Hyderabad studio is at Tellapur, and the work we have completed here so far is residential interiors, including two apartments at The Regent in Serlingampalli. The practice itself is fifteen years old and runs from Visakhapatnam, where the larger built portfolio sits.

We are straightforward about that balance because it affects what you should expect. A Hyderabad client gets a team that has designed across four states and a studio in the city for site coordination. What we do not claim is a decade of Hyderabad landmarks, because that is not what the portfolio shows.

Designing here is a different problem from designing on the coast. The heat is drier and harsher on west elevations, the water is hard enough to ruin fittings that were specified without a softening strategy, and much of the city sits on granite, which is excellent to found on until it needs breaking. We have set out the comparison in this article.
